# D2 Cardiogenic shock, cardiac arrest and mechanical circulatory support: evidence landscape

Written 24 October 2026.

## Scope and data basis

This landscape is drawn from the appraised subset of the store: 99 Tier A and B records tagged D2 (about 1,000 of 51,000 stored records across all domains have been appraised, and the backlog is still being worked through), 144 trial registry entries tagged D2, and the monthly counts file. Tier A and B selection favours target- and general-tier journals, so registry and observational work in specialty journals is under-represented. Some appraised records are tagged D2 as a secondary domain and belong mainly to D1, D3, D4 or D6; they are used here only where they bear on shock, arrest or mechanical support. Guideline extracts have not been retrieved, so no guideline class or level is stated, and all equipoise judgements rest on trial evidence only. The registry entries in the trials file carry no recorded primary outcome, so ongoing trials are listed by title, enrolment and primary completion date as registered.

## Settled questions

- Culprit-lesion-only PCI as the initial strategy in multivessel disease with infarct-related shock: CULPRIT-SHOCK, 706 patients, 30-day death or renal replacement therapy 45.9% versus 55.4% (PMID 29083953); 1-year death 50.0% versus 56.9% (PMID 30145971). Observational meta-analyses that pointed in different directions (PMID 28703046, PMID 29146672) are superseded.
- Routine intra-aortic balloon pump in AMI-CS: long-term follow-up of 600 patients, mortality 66.3% versus 67.0% (PMID 30586721).
- Routine early VA-ECMO in infarct-related shock: ECLS-SHOCK, relative risk 0.98 (95% CI 0.80 to 1.19) for 30-day death (PMID 37634145); ECMO-CS, hazard ratio 0.72 (95% CI 0.46 to 1.12) (PMID 36335478); four-trial IPD meta-analysis, odds ratio 0.93 (95% CI 0.66 to 1.29), with more major bleeding (PMID 37643628).
- Microaxial flow pump versus standard care in STEMI-related shock: 360 randomised, death 45.8% versus 58.5%, hazard ratio 0.74 (95% CI 0.55 to 0.99) (PMID 38587239). This is one trial, so the headline result is established but its generality is contested (below).
- Immediate versus delayed angiography after arrest without ST elevation: COACT (PMID 30883057), a second randomised trial (PMID 34459570) and COACT 5-year follow-up, 54.8% versus 51.8% alive (PMID 41636663) found no benefit of routine immediate angiography; the network meta-analysis found the apparent benefit confined to non-randomised studies (PMID 36300362).
- Milrinone versus dobutamine in shock: relative risk 0.90 (95% CI 0.69 to 1.19) (PMID 34347952).
- Moderate hypothermia in shock: 30-day death 42% versus 51%, adjusted odds ratio 0.71 (95% CI 0.45 to 1.13) (PMID 35103766).
- Levosimendan to facilitate ECMO weaning: successful weaning 68.3% in both arms (PMID 41324946).

## Contested questions

- Temporary mechanical support overall: the nine-trial IPD meta-analysis gave a hazard ratio of 0.87 (95% CI 0.74 to 1.03) for 6-month mortality, with benefit reported only in a selected subgroup and more bleeding and vascular complications (PMID 39236726). This sits beside the positive DanGer Shock result.
- Who benefits from the microaxial pump: exploratory age analysis, odds ratio 0.45 under 77 years versus 1.52 at 77 or over, P for interaction 0.028 (PMID 39551167); sex analysis, hazard ratio 1.01 in women versus 0.66 in men, P for interaction 0.18 (PMID 41222528); renal replacement therapy used in 42% versus 27% (PMID 39462276); a national registry in which 35.6% of Impella-treated AMI-CS patients met DanGer Shock criteria (PMID 41078250).
- Left ventricular unloading during VA-ECMO: observational meta-analysis risk ratio 0.79 (PMID 30765031), matched cohort hazard ratio 0.79 (PMID 33032450) and ELSO registry adjusted odds ratio 0.84 (PMID 35361346), against a 116-patient randomised trial with hazard ratio 1.02 (PMID 37850383).
- Extracorporeal CPR in refractory arrest: a 30-patient phase 2 trial favoured early ECMO (PMID 33197396), whereas the Netherlands trial (160 patients, odds ratio 1.4, 95% CI 0.5 to 3.5; PMID 36720132), a single-centre trial stopped for futility (PMID 35191923) and a registry (PMID 31670793) did not show a clear benefit.
- Microaxial support in high-risk PCI: the win ratio was 0.85 (95% CI 0.63 to 1.15) (PMID 41910380) while earlier national data associated Impella use with higher death and bleeding (PMID 31735078).
- Early LV unloading without shock in anterior STEMI: infarct size did not differ (PMID 42029358), following a small feasibility trial (PMID 30586728).
- Timing of complete revascularisation in STEMI, relevant to shock only as a boundary question: non-inferiority not shown (PMID 40902612) and worse outcomes with immediate revascularisation in heart failure on subgroup analysis (PMID 41493330). This overlaps the owner's active reconstructed-IPD network meta-analysis.

## Open questions

- Mechanical support in heart failure-related shock: a 101-patient balloon pump trial was neutral (PMID 40162941); the larger randomised unloading trial in this population is registered but unreported.
- Pump benefit in NSTEMI-related shock, shock with arrest and mixed shock, where outcomes differ in registry data (PMID 41078250) and no randomised comparison exists.
- Choice of first-line vasoactive agent: epinephrine was stopped early for refractory shock (PMID 29976291) and no adequately sized comparison has reported.
- Escalation, de-escalation and weaning of temporary support: addressed by consensus statements (PMID 35862152, PMID 42870411) rather than trials.
- Shock severity staging and trajectory: SCAI staging validated in cohorts (PMID 31548097, PMID 35835491) and serial assessment (PMID 39217545); no trial tests stage-directed therapy.
- Organisation of care: shock teams were associated with lower CICU mortality (PMID 34556316); structure and training are covered by statements (PMID 39945062, PMID 40249352).
- Shock in older adults, addressed by a scientific statement (PMID 38406869) without randomised data.
- Long-term outcomes of AMI-CS survivors: 5-year mortality 58.9% (PMID 37648357).
- Immediate angiography subgroups after arrest (shockable rhythm, ischaemic features) and long-term outcomes.
