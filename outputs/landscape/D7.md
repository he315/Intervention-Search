# D7 Type 2 MI and myocardial injury: evidence landscape

Drawn 24 October 2026 from the appraised subset only. Guideline extracts have not yet been retrieved, so no guideline class or level is stated anywhere in this file.

## Scope and data basis

The landscape rests on 44 appraised records for D7 (state/synth/D7_records.jsonl; 43 Tier B and 1 Tier A), a small part of the roughly 51,000 stored records across all domains, because the appraisal backlog is still being worked through. It covers type 2 MI, acute and chronic non-ischaemic myocardial injury, perioperative MI and myocardial injury after noncardiac surgery (MINS), and troponin pathways insofar as they set invasive management. Ninety-nine ClinicalTrials.gov or ISRCTN records were supplied (state/synth/D7_trials.jsonl); many of these are perioperative anaesthesia, haemodynamic or cardiac surgery trials that match the query only loosely and are not listed below. Study findings are given as reported in the abstract of the cited PMID. Of the 44 records, 18 are randomised trials or their secondary analyses, 3 are consensus or scientific statements, 2 are meta-analyses and the remaining 21 are cohort or registry studies. A short note on the data: the trend file for D7 holds rows only from January 2016 to October 2019, so the later trend cannot be described.

## Settled questions

- Type 2 MI carries a prognosis that is not benign. 1-year mortality was 13.8% after type 2 MI versus 9.4% after type 1 MI in an emergency department cohort (PMID 29020401); an adjudicated young-adult cohort reported higher all-cause mortality after type 2 than type 1 MI (hazard ratio 1.8, 95% CI 1.2 to 2.7; PMID 32138959); an individual patient data meta-analysis of 9 cohorts reported a MACE subdistribution HR of 3.36 (95% CI 2.92 to 3.86) for type 2 MI against no myocardial injury (PMID 41553312). These are observational associations.
- Perioperative troponin elevation is common and prognostically important. A 30-day mortality HR of 4.69 (95% CI 3.52 to 6.25) for an absolute hsTnT change of 5 ng/L or more was reported in 21 842 patients (PMID 28444280); perioperative myocardial injury occurred after 16% of 2546 surgeries with an adjusted HR of 2.7 (PMID 29203498). The two registry-derived hazards are associations.
- Implementing high-sensitivity troponin raises the number of type 2 MI and myocardial injury diagnoses without a measured change in treatment or outcome in the stepped-wedge High-STEACS cohort (PMID 31587565; PMID 30170853; five-year follow-up PMID 38011922).
- Safe early rule-out with a single low troponin or 0/1-hour algorithm is diagnostically well supported (PMID 31242362; PMID 37350492), and accelerated pathways shortened length of stay in randomised comparisons (PMID 37477353; PMID 42895015).
- Remote ischaemic preconditioning did not reduce myocardial injury after noncardiac surgery in PRINCE (relative risk 1.02, 95% CI 0.88 to 1.18; PMID 40511609), and ivabradine did not (relative risk 1.12, 95% CI 0.92 to 1.37; PMID 40884771). Colchicine did not reduce MINS in the thoracic surgery COP-AF trial (HR 0.89, 0.76 to 1.05; PMID 37640035).

## Contested questions

- Safety of 0/1-hour hs-cTnT rule-out. The randomised trial in PMID 31478763 found non-inferiority at 30 days (incidence rate ratio 1.06, 0.53 to 2.11), whereas the 12-month report from the same randomisation found all-cause death or MI of 5.0% versus 3.8% (HR 1.32, 95% CI 0.95 to 1.83; PMID 33998255). A separate stepped-wedge trial did not show 30-day non-inferiority for safety despite shorter stay (PMID 33752439).
- Single-threshold choice. Negative predictive value was reported as 99.8% at under 5 ng/L and 99.9% at under 2 ng/L (PMID 31475856), while sensitivity at 2 ng/L was 99.4% in early presenters (PMID 37350492); a 2026 head-to-head found higher sensitivity for the ESC 0/1h algorithm but fewer patients ruled out than High-STEACS 0/2h or 0/3h (PMID 41706062).
- Pharmacological treatment of established MINS. Dabigatran lowered the composite primary efficacy outcome (HR 0.72, 95% CI 0.55 to 0.93; PMID 29900874), but this is a single trial in 1754 patients and no confirmatory trial appears in the subset.
- Whether treatment of type 2 MI should follow type 1 principles. Coronary disease was found in 68% of 93 imaged patients, obstructive in 30% (PMID 35341327), and in 92% by CTA with obstruction in 26% (PMID 37777947), yet transfusion strategy effects by MI type did not show a significant interaction (P for interaction 0.16; PMID 39206549).

## Open questions

- Whether any therapy, including coronary evaluation, changes outcomes after type 2 MI. No randomised trial for type 2 MI appears in either input file.
- Whether routine postoperative troponin surveillance with a protocolised response lowers cardiovascular events; the registry-linked cohort in PMID 42880712 (adjusted HR 1.34 for 30-day mortality after type 1 perioperative MI) describes burden, not an intervention.
- Whether clinical-plus-troponin machine learning scores (PMID 37169863) change management or outcome compared with fixed thresholds.
- Optimal definition of prognostically relevant periprocedural injury after PCI (PMID 34059914; PMID 39968630; meta-analysis PMID 38742545).
- Whether acute and chronic myocardial injury warrant a defined care pathway, given the stepped-wedge finding of unchanged outcomes with sex-specific thresholds (PMID 31623760).

## Evidence trend

The trend file for D7 holds monthly counts only from 2016-01 to 2019-10. Annual totals (all designs): 366 in 2016, 355 in 2017, 403 in 2018 and 347 in 2019 (January to October only). Randomised trial counts were 32, 17, 23 and 14; meta-analyses 3, 11, 12 and 8; guidelines 2, 1, 4 and 2; observational 87, 97, 113 and 98. Over these four years the D7 output was flat, and no growth in randomised or synthesis output is visible. Counts for 2019-11 to 2026-09 are absent and need to be recounted before a ten-year trend can be stated.
