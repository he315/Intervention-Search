# D4 Mitral intervention: evidence landscape

## Scope and data basis

This landscape covers transcatheter and surgical intervention for mitral regurgitation (primary, secondary, atrial functional and acute post-infarction), transcatheter mitral valve replacement and mitral valve-in-valve. It is drawn from the appraised subset only: 47 Tier A and B records for D4 in state/synth/D4_records.jsonl, out of roughly 51,000 stored records across all domains, with the appraisal backlog still being worked through. Statements about what is settled or open therefore reflect that subset and must be checked against the full store before any question is promoted. Trial information comes from state/synth/D4_trials.jsonl (598 registry records, most of them unrelated to mitral disease; about 33 were relevant on title screening). Guideline extracts have not been retrieved, so no guideline class or level is stated here and guideline position is unverified throughout. Trial names are used only where they appear in the stored records.

## Settled questions

- Transcatheter edge-to-edge repair added to guideline-directed medical therapy in heart failure with selected secondary MR reduced heart failure hospitalisation (PMID 30280640: annualised rate 35.8% versus 67.9% per patient-year, HR 0.53, 95% CI 0.40 to 0.70) and, at five years, all-cause mortality (PMID 36876756: 57.3% versus 67.2%, HR 0.72, 95% CI 0.58 to 0.89). Durability and health status are supported by PMID 33632476 and PMID 30894288.
- A second randomised trial in secondary MR, in patients with heart failure and moderate to severe MR, reduced first or recurrent heart failure hospitalisation or cardiovascular death at 24 months (PMID 39216092: rate ratio 0.64, 95% CI 0.48 to 0.85).
- Concomitant tricuspid annuloplasty at the time of surgery for degenerative MR reduced the primary event rate (PMID 34767705: 3.9% versus 10.2%, relative risk 0.37, 95% CI 0.16 to 0.86), at the cost of more permanent pacemaker implantation (14.1% versus 2.5%).
- Transcatheter valve-in-valve in failed mitral bioprostheses has acceptable early outcomes in contemporary registry data (PMID 39101203), although this is observational evidence without a comparator.

## Contested questions

- Why the first two randomised trials of TEER in secondary MR disagreed. PMID 30145927 found no difference in the 12-month primary outcome (54.6% versus 51.3%, OR 1.16, 95% CI 0.73 to 1.84), whereas PMID 30280640 found a large benefit. Registry work on trial-eligible and trial-ineligible patients (PMID 34886961, PMID 37730284, PMID 38267110) and a transportability analysis (PMID 42233929, JACC, 6 October 2026) describe the populations, but no randomised evidence exists for patients outside the COAPT criteria.
- TEER versus surgery in secondary MR. PMID 39216093 (210 patients) reported a primary end point difference of -6 percentage points (95% CI -17 to 6), which does not exclude either strategy.
- TEER versus surgery in primary MR. Observational comparisons exist (PMID 38243821, French nationwide data: cardiovascular death HR 0.685, 95% CI 0.563 to 0.832, all-cause death HR 0.967), with randomised trials still under way.
- Moderate ischaemic MR at the time of CABG. PMID 27040451 found no difference in death at two years (HR 0.90, 95% CI 0.45 to 1.83) but less residual MR with the combined procedure; clinical benefit remains unclear.
- Management of MR shortly after myocardial infarction. PMID 34463727 (471 patients) associated intervention with lower mortality than conservative treatment (adjusted HR 0.28, 95% CI 0.18 to 0.46) but is confounded by selection for intervention.
- Interpretation of device success in cardiogenic shock. PMID 36126766 (3,797 patients) associated device success with lower one-year mortality (adjusted HR 0.49, 95% CI 0.41 to 0.59), a comparison vulnerable to survivor bias.

## Open questions

- Whether asymptomatic severe degenerative MR should be treated early. PMID 40799133 (single-centre prospective cohort, 1063 patients) associated early surgery with lower cardiovascular mortality (HR 0.17, 95% CI 0.07 to 0.40); the registered randomised trial reports in 2033.
- TEER for atrial functional MR. Observational data only (PMID 40629531, HR 0.65, 95% CI 0.43 to 0.99), with several small trials registered.
- TEER in patients outside trial criteria (advanced heart failure, right ventricular dysfunction, severe pulmonary hypertension; PMID 33243380, PMID 34886961, PMID 38267110) where outcomes are worse and benefit is unmeasured.
- Heterogeneity of treatment effect for TEER in secondary MR across the three randomised trials; no individual-level pooled analysis appears in the appraised subset.
- Transcatheter mitral valve replacement for patients unsuitable for surgery or TEER and for annular calcification: single-arm evidence only (PMID 41167201, primary endpoint 25.2%, 95% CI 20.6 to 30.6, against a performance goal of 45%; PMID 41194751; PMID 42873164; PMID 30357365 for worse outcomes after valve-in-mitral annular calcification).
- Anticoagulant choice after transcatheter mitral replacement (PMID 38199711, single-centre, 156 patients) and antithrombotic therapy after TEER (see trials below).
- Value of optimising heart failure therapy before TEER (PMID 37632738, registry association only).
- Residual and new-onset tricuspid regurgitation after mitral TEER (PMID 39873695).

## Evidence trend

The counts file for D4 currently runs only from January 2016 to November 2019 (47 months), because the trend retrieval has not yet been extended, so no 2020 to 2026 trend can be given. Annual totals of all D4 records were 691 (2016), 710 (2017), 739 (2018) and 756 (2019, January to November). Randomised trials numbered 11, 6, 6 and 12; meta-analyses 12, 18, 16 and 14; guidelines 1, 6, 1 and 2; observational studies 60, 80, 83 and 81. Design categories overlap and do not sum to the totals. Volume was growing slowly and randomised evidence was sparse before the 2018 trials. The appraised subset itself shows a later shift from registry reports towards randomised trials of secondary MR, TEER versus surgery, and single-arm transcatheter replacement studies.

## Candidate questions

- D4-C1: Reconstructed-IPD meta-analysis of the randomised TEER trials in secondary MR, testing effect modification and time-varying effects.
- D4-C2: Target trial emulation of TEER versus medical therapy in secondary MR patients outside trial criteria.
- D4-C3: Target trial emulation of TEER in cardiogenic shock with severe MR (overlaps the owner's acute MR and shock review; fails G4 only if no suitable data are reachable).
- D4-C4: Decision model with value-of-information analysis for early intervention in asymptomatic severe degenerative MR.
- D4-C5: Pre-registered synthesis timed to the primary MR TEER versus surgery readouts (fails G3 as written; a watch item for the congress workflow).
