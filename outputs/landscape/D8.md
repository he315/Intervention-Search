# D8 Antithrombotic therapy around interventions: evidence landscape

## Scope and data basis

This landscape covers domain D8 (SAPT, DAPT duration and composition, P2Y12 inhibitor monotherapy, de-escalation and guided therapy, high bleeding risk, antithrombotic therapy with oral anticoagulation, after TAVI, TEER and LAAO, LV thrombus, parenteral agents and factor XI inhibitors). It is drawn from the appraised subset only: 255 Tier A and B records (of the roughly 1,000 appraised across all domains and about 51,000 stored), together with 316 trial registry records. Gaps in the subset mean that some meta-analyses and trials published since 2016 will be absent, so every novelty claim below is provisional and must be checked against a full search before a protocol is registered. Guideline extracts have not been retrieved; no guideline class or level is stated here and equipoise is judged on trial evidence alone. Trial acronyms are used only where they appear in the records or registry fields. Registry fields for primary outcome are empty in the trials file, so primary endpoints are not given in the ongoing-trials table.

## Settled questions

- Dual therapy with a P2Y12 inhibitor and an anticoagulant, without aspirin, causes less bleeding than triple therapy in AF after PCI: PMIDs 28844193 (dabigatran, HR 0.52, 95% CI 0.42 to 0.63 for the 110-mg arm), 30883055 (apixaban versus VKA bleeding HR 0.69, 95% CI 0.58 to 0.81; aspirin versus placebo HR 1.89, 95% CI 1.59 to 2.24) and 27959713. An IPD network meta-analysis (PMID 42669062) found no difference in the primary efficacy outcome across strategies.
- Short DAPT followed by P2Y12 inhibitor monotherapy reduces bleeding without a measurable ischaemic penalty in broad PCI populations: PMID 39226909 (IPD of six trials; MACCE HR 0.91, 95% CI 0.78 to 1.07; BARC 3 or 5 bleeding HR 0.43, 95% CI 0.34 to 0.54), with the TWILIGHT trial (PMID 31556978) and PMID 31237644 as principal supporting trials.
- Abbreviated DAPT in high bleeding risk patients is non-inferior for net adverse events and reduces bleeding: PMID 34449185 (NACE 7.5% vs 7.7%; major or clinically relevant non-major bleeding 6.5% vs 9.4%).
- Chronic P2Y12 inhibitor (mostly clopidogrel) monotherapy is associated with fewer ischaemic events than aspirin monotherapy: PMIDs 34010616 (HR 0.73, 95% CI 0.59 to 0.90), 40902613 (IPD of seven trials, MACCE HR 0.86, 95% CI 0.77 to 0.96) and 40467090 (HR 0.77, 95% CI 0.67 to 0.89). The trials are open-label and predominantly East Asian, which limits generalisability but not the direction.
- Oral anticoagulant monotherapy is preferable to combination therapy in AF with stable coronary disease or remote stenting: PMIDs 31475793, 39225258 (HR 0.44, 95% CI 0.30 to 0.65), 41211917 and 40888725 (aspirin added to oral anticoagulation, adjusted HR 1.53, 95% CI 1.07 to 2.18 for the primary efficacy outcome).
- After TAVI without an anticoagulant indication, adding clopidogrel to aspirin increases bleeding (PMID 32865376, risk ratio 0.57, 95% CI 0.42 to 0.77 for aspirin alone) and rivaroxaban increased death or thromboembolism (PMID 31733180, HR 1.35, 95% CI 1.01 to 1.81).
- Rivaroxaban plus aspirin reduced the primary outcome relative to aspirin alone in stable coronary disease (PMID 29132879, HR 0.74, 95% CI 0.65 to 0.86).

## Contested questions

- Aspirin omission at primary PCI in STEMI. PMID 42670955 reported an HR of 1.34 (95% CI 1.02 to 1.75) for death, stroke or MI with monotherapy, PMID 41384891 reported an interaction by presentation (STEMI HR 1.60, 95% CI 1.14 to 2.24; P for interaction = 0.030), while PMID 40888726 reported 2.1% vs 2.2% in low-risk AMI. See candidate D8-C1.
- Prasugrel versus ticagrelor in ACS. PMID 31475799 (HR 1.36 for ticagrelor, 95% CI 1.09 to 1.70) conflicts with the registry-based PMID 42670968 (adjusted OR 0.90, 95% CI 0.77 to 1.06), and subgroup analyses by ACS type diverge (PMIDs 33115278, 33213722). See candidate D8-C2.
- Bivalirudin versus heparin in STEMI. PMID 36351459 reported 30-day primary endpoint 3.06% vs 4.39% (HR 0.69, 95% CI 0.53 to 0.91), whereas PMID 28844201 reported 12.3% vs 12.8% at 180 days. The pooled IPD (PMID 39384262) reported lower 30-day mortality (adjusted OR 0.78, 95% CI 0.62 to 0.99) with higher reinfarction (adjusted OR 1.30, 95% CI 1.02 to 1.65). A further IPD analysis in NSTEMI (PMID 37746717) exists, so a repeat synthesis would fail the incremental gate.
- Platelet-function or genotype-guided therapy. PMID 33865495 (RR 0.78, 95% CI 0.63 to 0.95 for MACE) and PMID 31479209 favour guidance, while PMID 32840598 reported HR 0.66 (95% CI 0.43 to 1.02) in loss-of-function carriers; several large trials are ongoing (NCT03783351, NCT05577988, NCT06283888).
- Extended DAPT beyond 12 months. PMID 42456136 (HR 0.82, 95% CI 0.69 to 0.98) and PMID 42875834 (MI RR 0.66, 95% CI 0.51 to 0.85) favour extension for ischaemic events, set against clopidogrel or P2Y12 monotherapy evidence above.
- Anticoagulation strategy after TAVI in patients with an indication. PMID 34449183 (major bleeding HR 1.40, 95% CI 1.03 to 1.91 for edoxaban), PMID 35583186 (primary HR 0.92, 95% CI 0.73 to 1.16) and PMID 39216096 (bleeding 31.1% vs 21.3% with continuation) do not converge on one regimen. See candidate D8-C3.
- LAAO versus oral anticoagulation (D9, lower weight). PMID 41910347 reported a primary efficacy difference of 0.9 percentage points (95% CI -0.8 to 2.6) with lower non-procedure-related bleeding (HR 0.55, 95% CI 0.45 to 0.67); meta-analyses (PMIDs 42860016, 42898218) found no difference in stroke.

## Open questions

- Antiplatelet and anticoagulant therapy in spontaneous coronary artery dissection: observational only (PMIDs 34338759, 40049585); NCT07683923 is not yet recruiting.
- Antithrombotic therapy after transcatheter edge-to-edge repair: no randomised evidence in the subset; NCT06901466 and NCT07007143 are recruiting.
- Antithrombotic regimen after LAAO: registry data (PMIDs 35512858, 39197978) and a small randomised trial (PMID 41139402); NCT05960721 and NCT07125417 are recruiting.
- LV thrombus prevention after anterior MI: an AHA scientific statement (PMID 36106537) is in the store; NCT06742567 is recruiting.
- Factor XI inhibitors in interventional populations: a phase 2 trial after MI (PMID 36030390) and a periprocedural analysis (PMID 40499984); NCT05754957 has completed primary data collection and is the principal readout.
- Antiplatelet duration after drug-coated balloon PCI: one randomised trial in the subset (PMID 40164448); several trials are registered (NCT07879430, NCT07398001, NCT07587099).
- Antiplatelet duration in advanced CKD: NCT04708587 is recruiting; no randomised result in the subset.
- Antithrombotic therapy for myocardial injury after non-cardiac surgery beyond a single trial (PMID 29900874, dabigatran, HR 0.72, 95% CI 0.55 to 0.93).

## Candidate questions

- D8-C1: Aspirin omission at primary PCI in STEMI, updated IPD meta-analysis (gates G1 to G5 pass; G3 carries a stated risk from NCT06509893).
- D8-C2: Prasugrel versus ticagrelor in ACS, pooled analysis including SWITCH-SWEDEHEART (all gates pass).
- D8-C3: Antithrombotic therapy after TAVI, IPD network meta-analysis in two networks (all gates pass; novelty unverified outside the subset).
- D8-C4: Transportability of de-escalation trial results to unselected UK PCI patients (G4 fails as framed).
