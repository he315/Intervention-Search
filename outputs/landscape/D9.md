# D9 Adjacent interventions: evidence landscape

## Scope and data basis

D9 covers left atrial appendage occlusion (LAAO), patent foramen ovale (PFO) closure, catheter-directed therapy for pulmonary embolism, renal denervation, interatrial shunts and the coronary sinus reducer, monitored at lower weight. This landscape is drawn from the appraised subset only: 37 Tier A and B records (one Tier A, 36 Tier B) out of the stored D9 records, with the appraisal backlog still being worked. It uses 139 ClinicalTrials.gov and ISRCTN entries, many of which are unrelated to D9 (for example gastric surgery, labour induction and anaesthesia trials caught by broad queries) and are ignored. Guideline extracts have not been retrieved, so no guideline class or level is stated, and gate G2 for every candidate rests on trial evidence only. The primary outcome field is empty for nearly all trial entries in the file, so primary endpoints for ongoing trials are not given.

## Settled questions

- Surgical LAAO added to cardiac surgery in patients with atrial fibrillation lowered stroke or systemic embolism: 4.8% vs 7.0%, hazard ratio 0.67 (95% CI 0.53 to 0.85) in LAAOS III (PMID 33999547); the effect was consistent across oral anticoagulant groups after discharge (PMID 37732457).
- PFO closure reduces recurrent stroke overall in pooled randomised data: adjusted hazard ratio 0.41 (95% CI 0.28 to 0.60) across six trials, 3740 participants, with benefit varying by RoPE score and PASCAL category (PMID 34905030).
- Amulet and Watchman are similar in effectiveness and safety endpoints at implant and five years, with more procedural complications with Amulet (PMIDs 34459659, 39570242, 34747186).
- Renal denervation lowers blood pressure against sham across trials: ten sham-controlled trials, 2478 patients, 24-hour systolic reduction 4.4 mm Hg (95% CI 2.7 to 6.1) (PMID 39355923); see also PMIDs 34010611, 36853250 and 36130612. Event effects remain unknown (see open questions).
- Interatrial shunt devices did not improve outcomes in heart failure with ejection fraction of at least 40% (PMID 35120593), and the overall RELIEVE-HF result was neutral (PMID 39308371).
- Mechanical thrombectomy beat catheter-directed thrombolysis on a hierarchical composite in intermediate-risk pulmonary embolism, win ratio 5.01 (95% CI 3.68 to 6.97), without a difference in mortality or bleeding (PMID 39470698). Surrogate and composite endpoints dominate this field.

## Contested questions

- LAAO versus anticoagulation: LAAO was non-inferior for the efficacy composite in anticoagulation-eligible patients (3000 patients; PMID 41910347) and after ablation (PMID 39555822), but was not shown to be non-inferior to best medical care in high-risk patients (PMID 41849741). Three October 2026 aggregate meta-analyses disagree in emphasis, including an excess of ischaemic stroke with LAAO (RR 1.41, 1.07 to 1.85; PMID 42860016) against non-inferiority for bleeding and death (PMID 42862780) and no difference for hard endpoints (PMID 42898218).
- Surgical LAAO in patients without atrial fibrillation: OPINION did not significantly reduce stroke, TIA or cardiovascular death (hazard ratio 0.83, 95% CI 0.61 to 1.14; PMID 40888584), against the positive LAAOS III in atrial fibrillation.
- Interatrial shunts by ejection fraction: RELIEVE-HF reported opposite signals in reduced and preserved ejection fraction, with higher MACNE in HFpEF and fewer adverse cardiovascular events in reduced ejection fraction (PMIDs 39308371, 42886087).
- Post-LAAO antithrombotic therapy: a network meta-analysis found DOAC associated with lower mortality than VKA and DAPT with fewer thromboembolic events than SAPT (PMID 37611779), while a randomised comparison found short-term DOAC did not significantly reduce device thrombosis versus DAPT (PMID 41139402).

## Open questions

- Hard clinical endpoints after renal denervation; the evidence base is blood pressure only.
- Whether advanced therapy for intermediate-risk pulmonary embolism lowers death or decompensation beyond early surrogates, and which modality; reported trials use different comparators and composites (PMIDs 39470698, 41183181, 41910345, 42678032), and a registry shows a shift towards thrombectomy (PMID 41739022).
- Peri-device leak: transoesophageal leak was associated with thromboembolism (pooled OR 2.04, 95% CI 1.52 to 2.74), CT-detected leak was not (PMID 38088437); the management consequence is untested.
- Concomitant LAAO at TAVR was non-inferior to medical therapy (HR 0.86, 95% CI 0.60 to 1.22; PMID 37874908); the longer-term and mortality effect is open.
- Coronary sinus reducer: angina improved without a change in myocardial blood flow in a small blinded trial of 51 patients (PMID 38604209); a hard-outcome question is unaddressed.
- PFO closure for migraine: a pooled two-trial analysis of 337 subjects met three of four endpoints (PMID 33573735); trials such as NCT04946734 are registered.
- Placebo-controlled invasive interventions as a class: 10 of 30 trials (33%) showed superiority over placebo (PMID 32666097).

## Evidence trend

The counts file holds D9 rows for 2016 to 2019 only; no D9 rows exist from 2020 onward in the file, so the later trend cannot be described and the trend step should be re-run for D9. Annual totals, all designs: 560 (2016), 588 (2017), 673 (2018), 521 (2019). RCT counts were 21, 28, 19 and 5; meta-analyses 25, 26, 58 and 16; guideline items 11, 0, 4 and 5; observational 71, 74, 83 and 58. The 2019 fall is likely an artefact of the incomplete baseline for D9, which the specification starts from 2019 for RCTs, meta-analyses and guidelines, and should not be read as a real decline.
