# D5 Tricuspid intervention: evidence landscape

## Scope and data basis

This landscape is drawn from the appraised subset of the store: 26 Tier A and B records for D5 (state/synth/D5_records.jsonl), out of roughly 51,000 stored records across all domains, with the appraisal backlog still being worked. It also uses 42 ClinicalTrials.gov records (state/synth/D5_trials.jsonl), several of which are not tricuspid trials and are ignored here, and the monthly counts file. Guideline extracts have not been retrieved, so no guideline class or level is stated and equipoise is judged on trial evidence alone. The trials file carries no primary outcome text, so primary endpoints are not given below.

## Settled questions

- In symptomatic severe TR, tricuspid TEER improved the primary hierarchical composite against control in a randomised trial (PMID 39471883, win ratio 1.84, P < 0.0001, 572 randomised), supported by earlier randomised reports (36876753, win ratio 1.48, 95% CI 1.06 to 2.13; 39602173, composite score improved in 74.1% vs 40.6%).
- Transcatheter valve replacement favoured replacement over control on the primary composite (39475399, win ratio 2.02, 95% CI 1.56 to 2.62, 400 patients), with more severe bleeding (15.4% vs 5.3%) and new pacemakers; a post hoc analysis reported consistent results by baseline severity (40878717).
- Tricuspid-valve intervention in heart failure favoured repair over medical therapy (42670975, win ratio 2.42, 95% CI 1.76 to 3.33; 360 patients).
- Two-year follow-up of TEER showed a lower annualised recurrent HF hospitalisation rate than control (40159089, 0.19 vs 0.26 per patient-year, P=0.02).

## Contested questions

- Whether benefit extends to hard components: 36876753 reports that death or surgery and HF hospitalisation did not appear to differ, and 39471883 reports annualised HF hospitalisation of 0.17 vs 0.20 (P=0.40), whereas 42670975 reports a hazard ratio of 0.40 (95% CI 0.29 to 0.55) for death from any cause or HF hospitalisation.
- Whether surgery for isolated functional TR improves survival: 31071413 found no difference (HR 1.34, 95% CI 0.78 to 2.30) while 39212387 found similar 10-year survival overall (HR .97, 95% CI .88-1.08) but benefit in the low TRI-SCORE category.
- Concomitant tricuspid repair at mitral surgery: 34767705 reduced the primary event rate (3.9% vs 10.2%) but raised permanent pacemaker implantation (14.1% vs 2.5%).

## Open questions

- Heterogeneity of treatment effect by baseline risk, RV function and renal function (baseline creatinine and TAPSE were associated with 1-year mortality in 38759905).
- Transcatheter versus surgical intervention for isolated TR, with no comparative study in the subset.
- Concomitant mitral and tricuspid TEER, given worse outcomes with new-onset or residual TR after mitral TEER (39873695).
- Indirect comparison of TEER and replacement, which have different populations and safety profiles.
- Cost-effectiveness and value of information of tricuspid devices.
- Long-term durability and antithrombotic strategy after TTVR, with single-arm data only (37930776).
