# D1 Coronary intervention: evidence landscape

Prepared 24 October 2026.

## Scope and data basis

This landscape covers domain D1 (PCI technique and devices, intravascular imaging and physiology, calcium modification, left main, bifurcation, CTO, complex and high-risk PCI, PCI versus CABG, revascularisation in chronic coronary syndromes and ischaemic cardiomyopathy, periprocedural myocardial infarction). It is drawn from the appraised subset only: 555 Tier A and B records of the roughly 51,000 stored records, because the appraisal backlog is still being drained. Absence of a paper from this document therefore does not mean it does not exist. The subset comprises 41 Tier A and 514 Tier B records (primary RCT reports 288, RCT secondary analyses 31, IPD meta-analysis 36, meta-analysis 49, network meta-analysis 27, guideline or consensus 40, registry or cohort 75). The trial file holds 606 ClinicalTrials.gov entries, of which 560 are randomised.

Guideline extracts have not yet been retrieved. No guideline class or level is stated here, and the position of ESC and ACC/AHA documents on each question is unverified. Where a guideline or consensus document appears in the records it is listed only as a record. Settled, contested and open judgements below rest on the appraised trial and meta-analytic evidence alone. Numbers are copied from the stored abstracts and attributed to the PMID.

Overlap with the owner's active projects: the reconstructed-IPD network meta-analysis of non-culprit lesion management in STEMI covers complete revascularisation in STEMI, and anything on that topic here is flagged and not proposed. Left ventricular unloading and cardiogenic shock belong to D2.

## Settled questions

- Drug-eluting versus bare-metal stents: IPD meta-analysis of 20 randomised trials (26,616 patients) found a reduced primary outcome with DES (HR 0.84, 95% CI 0.78-0.90) and no difference in all-cause death (HR 0.96, 0.88-1.05) (PMID 31056295).
- Radial versus femoral access: IPD meta-analysis of 7 trials (21,600 patients) reported lower all-cause mortality (HR 0.77, 0.63-0.95) and major bleeding (OR 0.55, 0.45-0.67) with radial access (PMID 36036610).
- iFR versus FFR to guide PCI: two noninferiority trials met their margin (PMIDs 28317438, 28317458).
- FFR-guided versus angiography-guided PCI: IPD meta-analysis of 5 RCTs (2,493 patients) found one-year MACE of 12.1% versus 14.7% (HR 0.80, 0.64-0.99), driven by peri-procedural MI (PMID 40831380).
- Left main PCI versus CABG in low to intermediate anatomical complexity: IPD meta-analysis of four RCTs (4,394 patients) gave 5-year all-cause death of 11.2% versus 10.2% (HR 1.10, 0.91-1.32), with higher spontaneous MI (HR 2.35, 1.71-3.23) and repeat revascularisation after PCI (PMID 34793745). No interaction by diabetes was found (PMID 38465592).
- Multivessel disease, PCI versus CABG: pooled IPD from 11 randomised trials (11,518 patients) reported 5-year mortality of 11.2% after PCI and 9.2% after CABG (HR 1.20, 1.06-1.37) (PMID 29478841). FFR-guided PCI did not meet noninferiority to CABG at one year in FAME 3 (PMID 34735046).
- Culprit-only PCI in multivessel disease with cardiogenic shock: the composite of death or renal replacement therapy at 30 days was 45.9% versus 55.4% (RR 0.83) in the 706-patient randomised trial (PMID 29083953). Multivessel disease in STEMI without shock is covered by the owner's active project (IPD meta-analysis, PMID 41223860).
- First-generation bioresorbable scaffolds were not superior to metallic stents in routine PCI (target-vessel failure HR 1.12, 0.85-1.48, PMID 28402237).
- Angina relief: in the placebo-controlled trial of 301 patients PCI lowered the angina symptom score relative to placebo (OR 2.21, 1.41-3.47) (PMID 38015442).
- Ischaemic left ventricular dysfunction: PCI added to optimal medical therapy did not change the primary outcome (HR 0.99, 0.78-1.27; 700 patients) (PMID 36027563).

## Contested questions

- Routine revascularisation versus medical therapy in stable coronary disease. One meta-analysis of 14 trials (14,877 patients) found no reduction in death (RR 0.99, 0.90-1.09) (PMID 32794407); another of 25 trials (19,806 patients) found lower cardiac death (RR 0.79, 0.67-0.93) and spontaneous MI (RR 0.74, 0.64-0.86) with no all-cause mortality difference (PMID 34002203). The ISCHEMIA extended follow-up found all-cause mortality of 12.7% versus 13.4% (adjusted HR 1.00, 0.85-1.18) (PMID 36335918), the CTO subgroup showed fewer spontaneous MIs but more procedural MIs with the invasive strategy (PMID 40139890), and long-term FAME follow-up reported a win ratio of 1.25 (1.01-1.56) favouring PCI, driven by urgent revascularisation (PMID 41540107).
- Intravascular imaging guidance. Positive trials in complex lesions (PMIDs 36876735, 39236729, 37634149) and in ACS (PMID 38604212) contrast with a neutral OCT trial (HR 0.90, 0.67-1.19, PMID 37634188) and two 2026 IVUS trials (HR 1.25, 0.97-1.60 in complex high-risk PCI, PMID 41911016; HR 1.11, 0.87-1.42 in left main, PMID 41911017). Several meta-analyses pool the trials (PMIDs 37973170, 38401549, 38344859, 37995152, 42747372), and one reports a geographic interaction with benefit concentrated in Asian trial populations (PMID 42879937).
- Drug-coated balloon versus drug-eluting stent in de novo disease. DCB was comparable to DES for small-vessel disease at 12 months (MACE 7.5% versus 7.3%, PMID 30170854) and reduced MACE against bare-metal stents in high bleeding risk (PMID 31204115), but REC-CAGEFREE follow-up at 3 years favoured DES (DOCE 8.2% versus 5.0%, P=0.002, PMID 41194754); an IPD meta-analysis in small vessels was significant in one-stage but not two-stage analysis (PMID 39981922).
- Left main bifurcation technique. Double kissing crush reduced one-year TLF versus provisional stenting (HR 0.42, 0.21-0.85, PMID 29096915), whereas the European trials found no difference between stepwise provisional and systematic dual stenting (PMIDs 34002215, 39907022).
- Angiography-derived physiology. QFR-guided PCI reduced one-year events against angiography (HR 0.65, 0.51-0.83, PMID 34742368), yet QFR was worse than FFR in a European noninferiority trial (HR 1.63, 1.11-2.41, PMID 39488224), while two 2026 trials met noninferiority against pressure-wire FFR (PMIDs 41910382, 41910384) and one against IVUS (PMID 40174597).
- Operator and hospital volume. The British Cardiovascular Intervention Society database showed a negligible association with 30-day mortality (OR per 100 procedures 0.99, 0.93-1.05, PMID 29579219), whereas US registry-Medicare linkage found lower in-hospital death with higher operator volume (PMID 30586696).
- PCI versus CABG in older patients with complex disease. An observational cohort reported excess adjusted mortality with PCI in patients aged 74 years or older (HR 1.40, 1.10-1.79, PMID 27009629); the randomised SYNTAXES age analysis (575 elderly patients) found no difference at 10 years (HR 1.08, 0.84-1.40, PMID 34082905).
- PCI in patients undergoing TAVI. PCI reduced MACE against conservative treatment (HR 0.71, 0.51-0.99, PMID 39216095), but two 2026 trials found deferral or later PCI noninferior (PMIDs 41921523, 42670987). Domain D3 overlaps.
- Ischaemic cardiomyopathy. CABG was associated with lower long-term mortality than PCI in a registry cohort (adjusted HR 0.59, 0.45-0.79, PMID 39471463), while PCI added no benefit to medical therapy in the randomised trial (PMID 36027563); an IPD analysis found fewer events in the medical-therapy arm of one trial than the other (adjusted HR 0.60, 0.48-0.74, PMID 40048661).

## Open questions

- Preventive PCI of non-flow-limiting vulnerable plaque: one randomised trial of 1,606 patients reported primary outcome in 0.4% versus 3.4% at 2 years, with a composite driven by softer components (PMID 38604213); the earlier scaffold trial was small (PMID 33069847). Five further randomised trials are registered (table below).
- Intravascular imaging in ACS and non-complex lesions beyond the existing ACS trial: large trials report from 2030 onward (NCT07512778, NCT06227754, NCT07616024).
- Hard-outcome effect of CTO PCI: placebo-controlled data are symptom-only and small (50 patients, PMID 41999379); NOBLE-CTO and ISCHEMIA-CTO are registered.
- Calcium modification strategy: an orbital atherectomy trial did not improve stent area or target-vessel failure (PMID 40174596); intravascular lithotripsy has registry data (PMID 33069849) and two registered randomised trials (NCT07388030, NCT07340294). No comparative evidence on hard outcomes exists in the subset.
- Revascularisation in advanced CKD and dialysis beyond the existing trials (PMIDs 27012407, 32227756); IVUS-CKD is registered.
- Revascularisation choices in women and ethnic minority groups: the RECHARGE trials (NCT06399692, NCT06399705) report after 2033; pooled sex-specific evidence is IPD-based (PMID 36780380).
- Complete revascularisation in NSTEMI: CompleteNSTEMI and others are registered; this overlaps the owner's active non-culprit lesion project and is not proposed.
- Systems of care: facility access and outcomes in AMI (PMID 42862553), a quasi-experimental veterans analysis (PMID 40742582) and international variation in PCI use (PMIDs 35508312, 37014339).
- Left ventricular unloading in high-risk PCI: a 300-patient randomised trial was inconclusive (win ratio 0.85, 0.63-1.15, PMID 41910380). This belongs to D2.
- Optimal strategy in PCI versus CABG for older patients and in ischaemic cardiomyopathy: see contested items.

## Candidate questions

- D1-C1: Time-varying effects of revascularisation versus medical therapy on spontaneous MI and cardiac death in stable coronary disease (reconstructed-IPD network meta-analysis). All gates passed, with G3 and G5 caveats.
- D1-C2: Effect modification of intravascular imaging guidance by region, lesion complexity and ACS (IPD meta-analysis). Fails G3.
- D1-C3: Operator and hospital volume thresholds for complex PCI, using a natural experiment in a national registry. All gates passed.
- D1-C4: PCI versus CABG in patients aged 75 years or older with multivessel or left main disease (target trial emulation). All gates passed, with a G5 caveat.
- D1-C5: Preventive PCI for non-flow-limiting vulnerable plaque (prospectively registered synthesis timed to readouts). Fails G3.
