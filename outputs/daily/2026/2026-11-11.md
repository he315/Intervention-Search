## Tier B

- In a US claims cohort of patients with ACS treated with PCI, prasugrel was associated with lower MACE and MI than ticagrelor, with no difference in bleeding. PMID 43004149
- Pre-specified subgroup analysis of DAPA-TAVI showing dapagliflozin reduced death or worsening heart failure after TAVI in patients with diabetes, with a smaller non-significant effect without diabetes and no significant interaction. PMID 43004293
- Meta-analysis of mainly observational studies comparing catheter-directed thrombolysis with mechanical thrombectomy in intermediate or high-risk pulmonary embolism found no difference in mortality or major bleeding. PMID 43007443
- Meta-analysis of three randomised trials found that routine PCI before or with TAVI did not reduce death, MI or stroke but increased major bleeding while reducing repeat revascularisation. PMID 43008125
- ESC Acute CardioVascular Care Association clinical consensus statement on structured early ambulatory follow-up after admission to an acute cardiac care unit, including after acute coronary syndromes. PMID 43008190
