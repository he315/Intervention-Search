## Tier A

### Long-term outcomes after percutaneous coronary intervention with drug-eluting stents versus coronary artery bypass grafting for left main coronary artery disease: a prospective analysis of the multicentre IRIS-MAIN registry.

Wee et al., Lancet (London, England), 3 November 2026; PMID 42976899; DOI 10.1016/s0140-6736(26)02104-5

Design and size: Non-randomised registry analysis with target trial emulation; 5379 patients underwent coronary revascularisation through either PCI with drug-eluting stents (3836 [71·3%]) or CABG (1543 [28·7%]); median follow-up 10·0 years.

Primary result as reported: In the overlap-weighted population, no statistically detectable difference in 10-year weighted Kaplan-Meier rates of all-cause mortality was observed between PCI and CABG (24·0% vs 22·9%; overlap-weighted hazard ratio 1·00 [95% CI 0·87-1·14]; p=0·99).

Why it matters: Large long-term emulated-trial comparison for left main disease in a general-tier journal; informs the PCI versus CABG debate but covers all ages and Asian registries, not the older-patient population of Q-004.

Main limitation: Observational design with adjustment for measured covariates only; the authors state that the confidence interval does not exclude benefit or harm with either strategy and that equivalence cannot be established.

Linked questions: Q-004
