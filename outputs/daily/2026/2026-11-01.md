## Tier A

### Transcatheter Aortic Valve Replacement in Pure Aortic Regurgitation: The Randomized SEASON-AR Clinical Trial.

Gao et al., Circulation, 31 October 2026; PMID 42965504; DOI 10.1161/circulationaha.126.082827

Design and size: Open-label randomised trial, 210 patients at 18 centres in China (TAVR n=109, GDMT n=101).

Primary result as reported: Kaplan-Meier estimate of the primary end point 8.3% vs 24.8%; hazard ration [HR], 0.31 [95% CI, 0.14-0.66]; P=0.001, driven primarily by a reduction in heart failure rehospitalization (3.7% vs 21.3%; HR, 0.16 [95% CI, 0.06-0.47]). Major or life-threatening bleeding and new permanent pacemaker implantation were higher with TAVR.

Why it matters: First adequately powered randomised evidence for TAVR in a population otherwise limited to medical therapy, with direct bearing on guideline recommendations for pure aortic regurgitation.

Main limitation: Open-label design, single-country investigator-initiated trial using one dedicated device (VitaFlow), with early excess bleeding and pacemaker implantation.
