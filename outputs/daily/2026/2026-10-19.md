### Temporary mechanical circulatory support in cardiogenic shock.

Chieffo et al., European heart journal, 14 October 2026; PMID 42870411; DOI 10.1093/eurheartj/ehag708

Design and size: Clinical consensus statement; no sample size.

Primary result as reported: The abstract does not report an effect estimate.

Why it matters: Sets contemporary guidance on device selection, escalation, de-escalation and shock team systems after recent microaxial pump and VA-ECMO trials.

Main limitation: Expert consensus built on a small number of randomised trials in a heterogeneous syndrome.
