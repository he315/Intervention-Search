## Tier A

### Transcatheter Edge-to-Edge Repair for Severe Isolated Tricuspid Regurgitation After Extended Follow-Up: The TRI-FR Randomized Clinical Trial.

Donal et al., Circulation, 23 November 2026; PMID 43074159; DOI 10.1161/circulationaha.126.080397

Design and size: Prespecified secondary analysis of a multicentre RCT (NCT04646811); 300 patients randomised (152 T-TEER plus OMT, 148 OMT alone); median follow-up 30.8 months.

Primary result as reported: First composite of HFH, tricuspid surgery or cardiovascular death: hazard ratio, 0.56 [95% CI, 0.36-0.88]; cardiovascular death or tricuspid surgery: hazard ratio, 0.75 [95% CI, 0.33-1.71]; recurrent HFH: rate ratio, 0.52 [95% CI, 0.32-0.84].

Why it matters: First randomised evidence that tricuspid TEER reduces clinical events beyond symptoms over about 2.5 years, relevant to guideline class for T-TEER and to pooled analyses of tricuspid trials.

Main limitation: Open-label trial of modest size with a composite driven by heart failure hospitalisation; cardiovascular death or surgery alone did not differ, and the landmark analysis is conditional on 12-month survival.
