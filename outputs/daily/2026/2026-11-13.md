## Tier B

- In a prespecified analysis of SENIOR-RITA, an invasive strategy did not reduce cardiovascular death or MI among older NSTEMI patients with cognitive impairment, with no interaction by cognition. PMID 43016171
- In a multicentre registry linked to Medicare claims, DAPT after Watchman FLX was associated with lower MACE and major bleeding than pooled DOAC-based regimens, driven by the comparison with DOAC plus antiplatelet therapy. PMID 43018051
- In the ELSO registry, higher hospital ECMO volume was associated with modestly lower adult mortality, while spreading ECMO across many ICUs was associated with higher mortality. PMID 43018409
- A prespecified sex subanalysis of the OPTION trial found LAAC and oral anticoagulation after AF ablation had similar efficacy in men and women, with a larger bleeding reduction from LAAC in men. PMID 43018504
- An IPD meta-analysis of CLOSURE-AF and PRAGUE-17 in patients at high stroke and bleeding risk found no difference between LAAC and contemporary medical therapy for efficacy or major bleeding, with excess early bleeding after LAAC. PMID 43018508
- Five-year follow-up of the prematurely stopped ASAP-TOO trial in OAC-unsuitable AF found LAAC numerically favourable for thromboembolism but imprecise, with more early major bleeding. PMID 43018511
- German multisociety consensus update defining structural, procedural and clinical requirements for ECPR programmes in refractory cardiac arrest. PMID 43018718
- A design-stratified meta-analysis of native-vessel versus bypass-graft PCI after CABG found observational data favouring native PCI but the one randomised trial, PROCTOR, favouring graft PCI at one year. DOI 10.21203/rs.3.rs-10689689/v1 (preprint)
