## Tier A

### Transcatheter Edge-to-Edge Repair for Severe Isolated Tricuspid Regurgitation After Extended Follow-Up: The TRI-FR Randomized Clinical Trial

Donal et al., Circulation, 23 November 2026; DOI 10.1161/circulationaha.126.080397

Design and size: Prespecified extended follow-up of a multicentre randomised trial (NCT04646811); 300 patients randomised (152 to T-TEER plus OMT and 148 to OMT alone); median follow-up 30.8 months.

Primary result as reported: First composite event (HFH, tricuspid surgery or cardiovascular death): hazard ratio, 0.56 [95% CI, 0.36-0.88]; cardiovascular death or tricuspid surgery: hazard ratio, 0.75 [95% CI, 0.33-1.71]; recurrent HFH: Lin-Wei-Yang-Ying rate ratio, 0.52 [95% CI, 0.32-0.84].

Why it matters: Randomised evidence from Tri.fr that tricuspid TEER reduces clinical events over longer follow-up, not only symptoms, which changes the pooled event count for any synthesis of the tricuspid trials and bears directly on Q-020.

Main limitation: Open-label trial with a secondary analysis of extended follow-up; the effect is driven by heart failure hospitalisation, a physician-adjudicated endpoint open to ascertainment bias, and cardiovascular death or surgery did not differ.

Linked questions: Q-020
