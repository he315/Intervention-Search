## Tier B

- An individual patient data meta-analysis of randomised MCS trials in AMI cardiogenic shock found no overall sex-specific effect, but in DanGer Shock-like patients the mortality association differed between men and women. PMID 43036048
- A meta-analysis of randomised trials found that planned PCI around TAVI did not reduce mortality or major ischaemic outcomes but was associated with fewer later revascularisations and more bleeding. PMID 43036114
- Ten-year extended follow-up of the DECISION-CTO trial found no difference in the composite of death, MI, stroke or revascularisation between an initial CTO-PCI strategy and no CTO-PCI. PMID 43037535
