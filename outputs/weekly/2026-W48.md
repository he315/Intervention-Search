Covers records entered in PubMed or posted between 16 November 2026 and 22 November 2026, and register and trial changes in the same week. Rendered 23 November 2026.

## Summary

No Tier A records entered PubMed in the week to 22 November 2026, and weekly counts were below the trailing 12-week mean in most domains because that mean is inflated by the TCT and AHA supplement abstracts indexed in October and early November; tricuspid intervention (D5) was the only domain above its mean. The pooled analysis of the tricuspid TEER randomised trials scheduled for PCR London Valves on 22 November 2026 has not yet appeared in PubMed or in a public programme summary, so Q-020 is unchanged and will be checked again in the coming week. In the trial registry, FIRE (NCT03772743) moved its primary completion date to 16 November 2026 and SWAP-7 (NCT06691191) moved to April 2027. Separately, 261 guideline and consensus records that had been assigned Tier D for lack of an abstract were re-read and tiered; six current guidelines or consensus documents now carry Tier A as reference documents for the register gates. The next-eight-weeks calendar still lists some registry entries outside the cardiovascular scope, which reflects the breadth of the trial snapshot queries rather than in-scope readouts.
