# Adversarial review of Q-011

Reviewer read only the PICO. Every identifier below was retrieved in this session from the local store, PubMed E-utilities or ClinicalTrials.gov API v2, and titles, journals and dates are copied from those sources. Where only the title or a record summary was read, that is stated.

## Existing syntheses or analyses

No systematic review, network meta-analysis, IPD analysis or large registry or emulation study in a target or general journal (Circulation, JAMA Cardiology, Eur Heart J, JACC, NEJM, Lancet, JAMA, BMJ) was found for post-TAVI antithrombotic strategy across the whole question. A PubMed search restricted to those eight journals, 2024 to 2026, returned only trials and unrelated syntheses. The ground is nevertheless well covered by specialty and lower-tier journals, and the closest items are:

- 38081213, Eur Heart J Cardiovasc Pharmacother, 14 August 2024. "Antithrombotic therapy in patients after transcatheter aortic valve implantation: a network meta-analysis." Bayesian NMA of 11 RCTs (n = 6415), co-primary death and major bleeding, median follow-up 6 months, three trials with an OAC indication. SAPT ranked best for both. Overlaps heavily with Q-011 on design and outcomes, at trial level.
- 40286375, JACC Advances, May 2025. "Post-TAVR Antithrombotic Treatment in Nonindicated Patients: Updated Systematic Review and Network Meta-Analysis." Frequentist NMA, 11 studies (8 RCTs, 3 propensity-matched cohorts), 5,821 patients, searched to August 2024. SAPT reduced life-threatening or major bleeding against DAPT, OAC and OAC plus SAPT. Covers the non-AF half of Q-011.
- 39991882, Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 24 February 2025. "NOACs after TAVR: a network meta-analysis." Four RCTs, 4808 participants, searched to 7 October 2023, with and without an anticoagulation indication, GRADE applied. Covers the DOAC arms and both strata.
- 40615791, BMC Cardiovascular Disorders, 4 July 2025. NMA of five regimens, 11 studies, 6,547 patients, searched to November 2024.
- 41019885, J Soc Cardiovasc Angiogr Interv, 1 August 2025. Meta-analysis with trial sequential analysis of randomised trials (title only read).
- 33860685, J Am Heart Assoc, 4 May 2021. "Aspirin Alone Versus DAPT After TAVI: A Systematic Review and Patient-Level Meta-Analysis." Existing IPD precedent for SAPT versus DAPT, so the IPD angle is not new for that pair.
- Further NMAs and meta-analyses in the store: 36640149 (Eur Heart J Cardiovasc Pharmacother, 10 April 2023), 40416807 (Front Cardiovasc Med, 2025), 39329477 (Cardiol Rev, 26 September 2024), 31883333 (Eur J Cardiothorac Surg, 2020), 35654173 (Int J Cardiol, 2022). Meta-analyses of DOAC versus VKA in AF after TAVI include 35901908 (Int J Cardiol, 2022), 36030549 (Clin Cardiol, 2022), 35941300 (Eur J Clin Pharmacol, 2022) and 41571953 (Eur J Clin Pharmacol, 23 January 2026).
- Observational analyses: 40020964 (Am Heart J, July 2025, DOAC versus warfarin, STS/ACC TVT registry) and 39890602 (Eur Heart J Qual Care Clin Outcomes, 11 August 2025, Danish nationwide study of antithrombotic therapy after TAVI). Neither is a target-journal paper, and I read the titles only.
- 33822924, Eur Heart J, 14 June 2021. ESC Working Group consensus document on antithrombotic therapy in TAVI (title read). It sets the guidance that a new synthesis would be judged against.

None of the above used reconstructed IPD with landmark or restricted-mean analysis, none stratified formally by AF status in a single network, and none handled CT-defined leaflet thrombosis as a network outcome. That is the residual novelty, and it is narrow.

New trial evidence since those syntheses was searched to 3 months before the run date:

- 42669043, JAMA, 30 August 2026. ACASA-TAVI (NCT05035277). 360 participants aged 65 to 80, NOAC monotherapy versus ASA monotherapy for 12 months, in patients without an OAC indication. HALT 16.2% versus 28.6% (risk ratio 0.55; 95% CI 0.37 to 0.82). Co-primary safety composite 7.5% versus 10.6% (risk difference -3.3%; 95% CI -9.5% to 2.8%; P for non-inferiority < .001).
- 42669071, J Am Coll Cardiol, 30 August 2026. NOTION-4 (NCT06449469). 352 randomised, SAPT versus 3 months of DOAC then SAPT. HALT at 1 year 32.2% versus 28.3% (risk difference -3.9%; 95% CI -14.4% to 6.6%; P = 0.54). All-cause death, stroke or major or life-threatening bleeding 2.3% versus 8.2% (risk difference 5.9%; 95% CI 1.2% to 10.6%).

These two trials already reported, so the "pre-specified update at the next readouts" archetype has partly lapsed for the non-AF stratum. Both are small and report HALT as the primary endpoint, which is the one outcome no existing NMA pools.

No trial identified would be an adequately powered answer to the whole question within 24 months, but several (REAC-TAVI2, NAPT, plus ACASA-TAVI and NOTION-4 already out) will materially change the non-AF pooled evidence.

## Editor objections

1. Not new. Six or more network meta-analyses of the same trials exist, one in the Cochrane Library and one in JACC Advances. An editor at a parent journal would ask what a seventh adds beyond the new inputs.
2. Trial-level sparsity. With four or five RCTs per stratum, a network with roughly eight nodes (SAPT, DAPT, DOAC, VKA, CT-guided, OAC plus SAPT, no therapy) is poorly connected. Several comparisons rest on a single trial, and CT-guided therapy has no completed RCT in the network at all.
3. Heterogeneous follow-up and populations. Median follow-up differs (about 6 to 18 months, per 38081213 and 39991882), and ACASA-TAVI enrolled only ages 65 to 80 while the older NMAs had a mean age near 81. Transitivity is doubtful across these.
4. Leaflet thrombosis is a surrogate. HALT is subclinical, timing of CT differs, and NOTION-4 showed a 3-month effect that disappeared by 12 months with excess adverse events in the DOAC arm. A parent journal would not accept a surrogate as a decision endpoint.
5. The headline finding is probably stable. Existing syntheses consistently favour SAPT in non-AF patients, and the new trials do not obviously overturn that. A confirmatory result is hard to sell as a main article.
6. Prospective registration of an update "at each readout" is a protocol device, not a scientific contribution.

## Design objections

- Reconstructed IPD requires Kaplan-Meier curves with numbers at risk. Many of these trials report composite or bleeding endpoints without usable curves, and HALT is a binary imaging outcome at a fixed time that cannot be reconstructed as time-to-event. The proposed CT-defined leaflet thrombosis outcome therefore fits a standard aggregate-data network but not the time-to-event reconstruction.
- Landmark analysis at 30 days and restricted mean survival time rest on reconstruction accuracy that degrades for rare events (stroke, death in short trials) and for curves truncated at 6 months.
- REML with Hartung-Knapp on a sparse network with one trial per edge is poorly behaved. Between-study variance is not estimable for single-trial edges, and Hartung-Knapp with very few studies gives wide intervals that may add no information beyond the existing NMAs.
- Effect modification by AF status is a between-trial comparison (the trials enrolled one stratum or the other), not a within-trial interaction, so it is confounded by trial. True IPD from ATLANTIS, which stratified by OAC indication, would be needed and requires collaboration with trial investigators.
- Pooling an AF network (DOAC versus VKA, with or without antiplatelet) and a non-AF network (SAPT, DAPT, DOAC) into one NMA invites an invalid shared-comparator assumption. They are better analysed as two separate networks.
- A credible version needs true IPD from the main trials (consortium route), which would also allow the 30-day landmark and competing-risk analysis. A reconstructed-IPD NMA alone is unlikely to be cited by a guideline committee as decisive.

## Verdict

Survives with a narrower angle.

The question is not fully scooped: no target- or general-journal synthesis covers it, and no existing analysis combines AF stratification, time-to-event reconstruction and CT-defined leaflet thrombosis. It is however heavily overlapped at trial level by 38081213, 40286375, 39991882 and 40615791, the non-AF comparison (SAPT versus DAPT versus DOAC) is largely settled in favour of SAPT in their pooled results, and the two big new readouts (42669043, 42669071) have already occurred. The defensible angle is narrower and more demanding: true IPD, or reconstructed IPD where curves allow, for the AF or OAC-indication stratum and for leaflet thrombosis as a mediator of clinical events, with trial-level HALT data pooled separately as a binary outcome and the CT-guided strategy treated as a prospective update once NCT06168370 reports (December 2028). The all-strategy network should not be presented as the primary design.

Suggested changes to the gates and scores:

- G3 (not about to be answered): keep as pass only for the AF or OAC-indication stratum. For the non-AF stratum it should be marked at risk, because REAC-TAVI2 (NCT05283356, June 2027) and NAPT (NCT06007222, December 2026) read out within 24 months and the target-journal threshold of "materially changes the pooled sample" must be quantified. ACASA-TAVI (n = 360) and NOTION-4 (n = 352) add about 712 participants to roughly 6,400 to 6,500 in the existing NMAs, around 11%, which is not by itself material. The CT-guided arm cannot be answered by synthesis before NCT06168370 completes.
- G5 (not incremental): flag as borderline. The all-strategy aggregate network is a repeat of the same trials. It passes only if the design is changed to IPD or to the AF stratum with formal effect-modification and landmark analysis.
- Novelty: reduce from the presumed level to 2 or 3 of 5 for the full question, 3 for the AF and IPD angle.
- Equipoise: reduce for the non-AF comparison (SAPT is guideline-supported and preferred in the pooled results above), keep at 3 to 4 for AF or OAC-indication patients and for CT-guided therapy.
- Timeliness: keep, but reframe around REAC-TAVI2, NAPT and POP ATLANTIS rather than the readouts that have already occurred.
