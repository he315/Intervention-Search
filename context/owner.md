# Owner profile

Hussein. UK cardiology trainee and NIHR Academic Clinical Fellow (pre-doctoral) at Barts Heart Centre and Queen Mary University of London (William Harvey Research Institute), with clinical and research work at Harefield Hospital. Career aims: post-CCT fellowship in interventional cardiology and mechanical circulatory support, and a UK and European cardiogenic shock registry-embedded randomised trial platform modelled on SWEDEHEART and SCAAR.

## Methods the owner delivers
- Systematic reviews and meta-analyses, including network and component network meta-analysis, reconstructed individual patient data from published Kaplan-Meier curves, and prognostic and diagnostic accuracy meta-analysis (R, meta, metafor).
- Health economic decision modelling: patient-level Markov microsimulation with probabilistic sensitivity analysis.
- Registry-embedded trial methodology: specifying, validating and embedding randomisation in registries that others are building.
- Clinical trials training (Oxford MSc in Clinical Trials, place accepted).
- Prospective biomarker studies (principal investigator of a biomarker study at Harefield).
- UK Biobank analyses, including ACE inhibitors and SGLT2 inhibitors in aortic stenosis.

## What the owner wants from this system
Large, practice-changing questions that could stand as main articles in Circulation, JAMA Cardiology, the European Heart Journal or JACC. No quick wins. Both evidence synthesis and original research. Feasibility is reported for every question but is not used to filter out large questions.
